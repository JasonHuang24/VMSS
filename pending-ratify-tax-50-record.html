Founder ruling R17 — loop-termination rule for the Charter drafting; ratified (canon v22.5)

R17 (founder ruling): LOOP-TERMINATION RULE. An adversarial seat's output is unbounded by design, and what is bounded is the response function. This is the R9 principle, carried from a ballot to a Charter to make the arc terminable. For the Path 2 Charter the rule is pre-declared and cold. One regression pass runs against the amended draft, its findings are dispositioned on the record, and the founder's disposition is terminal. No later pass may reopen a disposition a prior pass closed. The bar for landing is stated in advance: zero standing SEV-1 findings, and every SEV-2 ruled on the record. One one-shot second-seat cross-check is permitted, scoped strictly to the institutional-design surface the first seat did not see. It may file fresh findings there and nowhere else, and it cannot reopen anything outside that scope. Ratified.
