1. **DS-06/DS-24: not applicable to this page.** The patch ruling targets layer-0.html (Darius Okafor, "The Explorer"); layer--1.html has no such vignette and was not in scope for it. No HOLD-DS24.
2. **Rafael "ascending" (left unchanged, flag for Jason).** «has not thought about ascending in three years» and the frozen Outcome 'No Ascension Pursued' imply an ascension option, but his reassignment is punitive and the page itself says punitive -1 placement is permanent with no pathway back (mobility is downward only). Read as his settled attitude, it contradicts nothing; read as an available choice, it conflicts. Per instructions, the wording was left as written.
3. **Rafael STI drop then reassignment (left unchanged, reading note).** 'His STI dropped from 68 to 41 in a single logged event. Layer reassignment to -1 followed within hours.' Placing the two sentences side by side can read as STI setting placement (against the ruling that STI never sets placement). The paragraph names the violation as the cause, so I kept the original sequence verbatim instead of recasting it.
4. **Children's standing right to relocate upward (no change).** «retain a standing right to relocate to Main Layer (0)» is an upward move, but it is a canon exception (layer-0.html, whitepaper) and not a conflict with the downward-only ruling for adult placement.
5. **STI indicator colours (pre-existing inconsistency, unchanged).** Monitoring says «predominantly orange and red»; STI Visibility says «most indicators are red». Both are left as written.
6. **Reading decisions.** 'The market is not grey' was read as "not a grey market" and rendered «The market is legitimate»; 'preferential compared to the local population' was rendered «compares favorably with the local population's». 'every interaction' became «daily interaction» (blanket assertion). Two balanced closers that carry doctrine were kept in plain form rather than cut: «The Balanced Layer earns its name by constraining both directions» and the page closer «-1 is a consequence state rather than a failure state».
7. **Length.** -6.2% overall, inside the 5-15% aim. The "Current tax frame" paragraph was left untouched because every token in it is a frozen figure or citation.
