## 1. Scores

- **Makes sense on first read: 6/10.** By the end I could describe the five rings, the two tracks and the money gradient. But the archive teaches all of it through about 93 stories stamped with some 25 doctrine versions, mixed in with many invented "allied nations" that change one rule each, so I had to work out the base rules myself by comparing stories.
- **Internally consistent: 5/10.** Most numbers hold up well (UBI cascade, revival odds, -3 tax, SCM arithmetic). But several core rules really do conflict: whether social breaches can move a ring, whether currencies convert, whether there are judges, whether prevention exists outside Sanctuary, and whether -3 and -2 are watched or enforced. About half of these carry no label that tells the reader which side governs.

## 2. What I now understand (about 150 words)

VMSS is a proposed civilization founded in 2026 (Y0). People join voluntarily, and it is now about 4.3 billion people living in five walled rings: +1 Sanctuary, 0 Main, -1 Noncompliance, -2 Violent Offense and -3 Terminal. A brain implant records conduct. A Social Trust Index (STI) scores social trust. It can move a person only between Main and Sanctuary ("phasing" at STI 85). Qualifying harmful acts are judged by AI (severity, pattern, reversibility) and lead to permanent "reassignment" downward. Sanctuary blocks harm before it completes (TIP). Main answers harm afterward. Further down, the institutions withdraw: UBI halves at each ring ($10k, $5k, $2.5k, $1.25k), revival odds worsen (1 in a million, then 1 in 10,000, then 1 in 1,000, then none in -3), and private justice takes over. A federal floor still binds every ring (no nuclear weapons, clean energy, no implant hacking, no sovereignty threats). Laws come from citizen petitions, expert drafting and population ratification. A Meritboard picks officials on merit, and there are no elections. Separate layer currencies and a savings mandate stop wealth from piling up.

## 5. Where a careful reader gives up or is misled, and three small fixes

**Where:** The worst point is the run of allied- and adjacent-nation stories (Softer Ring to Living Laboratory, about 15 long cards). Each changes one rule, and they are written in the same register as the VMSS cases. A reader skimming them will take the variant's rules for VMSS rules (for example, an equal UBI, human judges or recovery pathways). The place a careful reader is most likely to be misled, not just tired, is the trio of "verdict" passages that state rules flatly and conflict with other canon cards: currency non-convertibility [1794], "prevention existed only in Sanctuary" [1731] and "the only enforced prohibition is killing" [3772]. They are written as the doctrine's own answers, so a reader trusts them over the stories that contradict them.

**Three smallest changes:**
1. **Under the Trust Console intro ([57])**, add one sentence: "A long, uncorrected pattern of minor infractions is evaluated as a qualifying act and leaves track 1." Or, if that is wrong, correct The Threshold's key lesson [417]. Either way the two can no longer conflict.
2. **At the Surface Reader verdict ([1794]) or in index [399]**, add a one-line currency rule: "Layer currencies never trade between layers; the only conversion is one-way and downward, at the central bank's rate, on voluntary descent or for a downward visitor." This reconciles [840], [4467] and [6266].
3. **At the head of the "Civilizational" collection ([964]/[1909])**, add a label: "From here, most entries describe *other* gradient nations that changed one VMSS rule; their rules are not VMSS law." Then add to [249]: "Current doctrine: v__; cards stamped earlier may show rules since superseded even where no note says so."
