## 1. Scores

- **Makes sense on first read: 6/10.** The core shape (five rings, sorting by conduct, permanent descent, a trust score, backup bodies) comes through in the first two pages. But the reader has to carry dozens of LP numbers, article numbers, whitepaper section references and coined terms (TIP, SCM, Ceiling Seal, External Force Doctrine, Five Instruments, Heaven Layers) that these pages never explain, and the stories carry version stamps nobody explains.
- **Internally consistent: 7/10.** The numbers agree to a striking degree: UBI halving, tax cascade, populations summing to 4.3B, 32.5% eligibility = 1.4B, revival failure rates. The real disagreements are a dozen or so rule-level ones, mostly where a flat summary sentence drops a condition that a deeper page relies on, or where a story assumes mechanics that the rule pages forbid.

## 2. What I take away (about 150 words)

VMSS is a proposed voluntary civilization on one planet, founded in 2026 and described as it stands in the early 2300s, with about 4.3 billion people. It is split into five concentric rings walled 15 km high. Entrants with no qualifying criminal record start in Main (0), about 3B people with $10k/month UBI and post-scarcity tech. Sustaining a Social Trust Index of 85+ makes you eligible for Sanctuary (+1), where a mandatory implant stops harm before it completes. Brain implants and AR cameras record conduct. Harmful acts trigger automatic, permanent reassignment downward by severity: -1 for fraud/DUI-type breaches, -2 for predatory violence, -3 for murder, child rape or attacks on the system. Each lower ring has less state presence and more private order, half the UBI and half the top tax rate of the ring above, and its own non-convertible currency. Backup bodies revive the dead in every ring except -3. Nobody is elected: leaders come from a competence ranking (the Meritboard), and rules pass by 1% petition and then an 80% direct vote. People can also choose to live in lower rings, permanently or not.

## 3. Contradictions

1. **Murder victims always revive vs. only within enforcement reach.** why-vmss [174]: "Above -3, murder becomes structurally futile because the victim revives and the perpetrator faces reassignment". systems [420]: "In Main Layer, -1 and -2 ... Murder victims are revived in backup vessels at full fidelity". Against: layer--2 [99]: "An unfunded victim of criminal homicide is restored only where a responding unit performs the terminal sync capture ... nonexistent in the wilderness ... the victim's restoration is not [certain]". faq [110] hedges ("Within enforcement reach, the victim comes back"). *Governing order:* no passage is labelled as governing. The FAQ's hedge suggests the -2 page is right, but the two flat statements stand uncorrected. **Reader left to guess.**

2. **AR never triggers reassignment by itself vs. AR replaces the implant for people without one.** technologies [327]: "AR data corroborates evidence for acts that have breached reassignment thresholds but never independently triggers reassignment." Against: technologies [302]: "AR covers equivalent external observation for non-implant-bearers"; [332]: "Removing the implant opens no forensics gap"; and implants are optional in Main (technologies [61]). Read literally, someone without an implant who commits murder in Main could never be reassigned. **Reader left to guess.**

## 4. Confusions

**Resolved later**
- index [399] packs the glossary (TIP, phasing at 85, ladders of "tiers", the Y0 = 2026 dating) into one paragraph before the reader knows any of the systems. It makes sense only after layers/systems.
- "SCM" (why-vmss [138]) is unexplained until systems [283].
- "Ceiling Seal" (why-vmss [504]) is explained by why-vmss [537].
- "autoparenting" (layer-0 [61], "ascended from autoparenting") is explained by technologies [399].
- "funded vessel" (layer-plus1 [114]) is explained only at layer--2 [95] ("revival fees are paid up"). The upper-layer pages never say that Main residents pay vessel fees, or what happens if they don't.
- "Chief Architect" (systems [182]) is partly explained by faq [944] (author of the Preamble).
- Why someone in -3 who commits a further capital crime isn't reassigned: answered at layer--3 [92] ("there is no ring below").
- Systems [366] ("assigned along the layer gradient") vs layer-0 [51] ("default starting point for all voluntary entrants"): clarified by faq [220].
- Nuclear/hacking in -3: systems [480] and [484] say "VMSS does not intervene" and, in the same paragraph, that the federal floor "still enters". This is resolved within the paragraph but reads as self-contradiction at first.

**Never resolved**

- The LP numbers (LP-004.2, 006, 013, 062.2, 063, 064, 065, 068, 069, 071, 074, 076, 077, 079, 080, 081) and why-vmss [138]'s "both schedules certified in the 2294 Path 2 audit". Index [399] hints that LP means Law Polling, but "Path 2" and "both schedules" are never explained.
- "External Force Doctrine" (layer--3 [51], [60]), "Five Instruments" (technologies [302]), "resurrection tourists" (faq [230]), "cyberware-decompensation" (faq [280]), and whitepaper §5.5/§5.7/§5.8/§17.3.1/§17.1.5/§22.1 are all cited but never explained on these pages.
- "Heaven Layers" (plural) in systems [382], faq [79] and faq [169]. There is one Sanctuary ring, so the reader cannot tell whether SADs count as "layers".
- The federal floor's legal basis: "Article VI federal floor" (systems [480]) vs "Article XXV.IV" tracks (layer--3 [92], faq [966]). Are these the same instrument?
- UBI during elective residency "at the habitual-residence layer's rate (LP-081)" (systems [247]). Is a Main citizen living electively in -1 paid $10k or $5k? Never stated.
- -1 [97]: "The system does not proactively inform children of this right; discovery is organic". Yet every child has "an independent AI legal advocate from birth" (systems [370]) who "executes relocation" (layer--3 [64]). Does the advocate stay silent?
- Rafael (layer--1 [112], outcome "No Ascension Pursued" [109]) "has not thought about ascending in three years", which implies he could ascend. The same page says there is "no pathway back" ([95]). Is this a stale story or a figure of speech?
- technologies [288]: "The terminal layer's medical leakage floor persists at approximately 1% by 3000". -3 has "no institutional medical service" (layer--3 [63]), and the same paragraph says the lower-layer gap "does not close as technology advances". A 1% shortfall in a layer with no state medicine is unintelligible.
- AGIs can "ascend to Sanctuary" (faq [747]), but Sanctuary requires a neural implant for TIP (layer-plus1 [60]). How?
- The rings and permanent reassignment date from founding, but the walls are "a 22nd–24th-century build" (index [399], technologies [476]). What bounded the rings for the first century? This is never addressed.
- Text type. why-vmss is advocacy written as argument ("VMSS does. That alone puts it ahead"). The layer pages mix rule statements, social description, history (the Saurian Park, the 2093–2115 vessel exploit) and stories. Only the stories are labelled. It is hard to tell whether "Duels ... legal in -3" (layer--3 [111]) is law, customary practice, or the narrator's view. faq [634] calls it the "cooperative-tolerance framework", which helps only partly.

## 5. Where a reader gives up or is misled, and three small fixes

**Most likely failure points.**
1. The death rules. A reader who stops after why-vmss or systems will believe murder is always reversed above -3 and that unimplanted people are tracked by AR as fully as implanted ones. Both are wrong or conditional, according to -2 [99] and technologies [327].
2. The stories. They read as canon. A careful reader who meets doctrine notes on some stories will reasonably assume un-noted details (Elias's pre-bought compound, Rafael "ascending", "within hours") are current.
3. The citation fog. By systems and faq, nearly every paragraph leans on an LP number, an article or a § the reader cannot see. This is where a careful newcomer is most likely to give up.

**Three smallest changes.**
1. Under every "Doctrine Snapshot" stamp, add one line: "Illustration written under an earlier rule set; where it differs from the rules on this page, the rules govern." This gives the reader a governing order for contradictions 8, 13 and the Rafael confusion without rewriting the stories.
2. Add the condition to why-vmss [174] and systems [420]: "...the victim revives, if they hold a funded vessel or a responding unit reaches them in time (LP-065)." This fixes the site's most-repeated and most misleading summary claim.
3. At the first LP citation (why-vmss [138]), add the gloss: "LP-numbers are enacted federal laws, listed in VMSS Laws; you can read this site without them." It tells the reader the numbers are references, not prerequisites.
