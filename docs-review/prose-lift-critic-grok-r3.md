Recurring habits

1. A neater sentence after the reader already has the beat. Highest of what remains, and still small: "taken their labor with them" in Where the Maps End, and "You rise through the warmth..." in Maren Solvik.
2. Lines set into a balanced pattern. "Hers or theirs." "Honey for the warmth, copper at the spike, clean water for the calm." These show up in those same two stories. A reader hears the arrangement and continues.
