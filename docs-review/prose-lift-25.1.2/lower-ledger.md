# Prose lift 25.1.2: doctrine-fix pass, lower-layer and mechanism pages

Scope: fixes 1-7 of the 25.1.2 doctrine-fix patch (approved by Jason 2026-09-28), applied to copies of layer--3.html, layer--2.html, systems.html and roadmap.html in this folder. Only the numbered fixes were made. Every other byte is identical to the live page (checked by `lower-verify.mjs`). The length target does not apply to this patch.

Quote convention: «...» quotes are checked verbatim against the edited copy by `lower-verify.mjs`. "Before" quotes use plain double quotes and are the text as it stood on the live page.

## layer--3.html

### Fixes applied

**Fix 1: Backup Vessel Parity (25.0.1 minus3 ledger, flag 1).** Canon source: LP-004.2 (laws.html #code-lp-004-2), also stated correctly in this section's own framework paragraph: «death inside -3 is final for visitor and resident alike».

- Opener. Before: "has every right any -3 resident has — except one asymmetry the architecture did not initially anticipate. The visitor has backup vessel continuity. The native does not. Death is temporary for the visitor and permanent for the native." After: «Before LP-004.2 there was one asymmetry» ... «The visitor had backup vessel continuity. The native did not.» ... «Death was temporary for the visitor and permanent for the native». The paragraph already said the exploit came from «the civilization's early decades» and that «the doctrine now addresses» it, so the pre-law framing only needed the tense and an explicit anchor to the law.
- Exploit paragraph. Before: "The exploit is total. ... Imprisonment is futile — the visitor dies in the cell, revives in Main, and returns." After: «The exploit was total» ... «Imprisonment was futile — the visitor died in the cell, revived in Main, and returned». Every verb about the visitor's advantage is now past tense. Two present-tense statements were kept because they are still true: «-3's entire economic and social architecture assumes permanent mortality» and the parenthetical rule of the Colosseum «(could not lose what the Colosseum demands you risk)».
- Closer. Before: "trades in non-mortality-dependent markets is unaffected. The provision constrains only the activities where immortality creates structural competitive advantage over mortal residents." After: «non-mortality-dependent markets gives up only the vessel link, and only for the visit» and «removes only the structural competitive advantage immortality would create over mortal residents». Under terminal severance every visitor's link is suspended at entry, so "unaffected" and "where immortality creates" stated a live visitor vessel link in -3. The new wording follows LP-004.2's «severed for the duration of the visit and restored on exit».

**Fix 2: Wilderness hostage sentence (25.0.1 minus3 ledger, flag 2).** Canon source: STI and the public ledger run in -3; this page's own «The public ledger still travels with every resident» and «The implant still sits in the skull». Before: "A person with no implant, no district, no vouchers and no column of numbers anywhere". After: «A person with no district, no vouchers and no column of numbers in any association». "no implant" is dropped. "anywhere" becomes "in any association", which ties the column to the association reputation ledger defined in the paragraph before («That instrument is the reputation ledger, read backwards») rather than to the public ledger.
